### Inferences
- The split looks like written release versus spoken press conference. Two-decimal figures, 5,246 for ANI and BSLT appear in the articles that cite the ministry's material (Pontianak Post credits ATR/BPN); round figures, 5,244 and PSLT appear in the same-afternoon reports of the spoken remarks. detikFinance is a hybrid (BSLT, but 5,244). This is my reading, and the release itself was not retrieved.
- 1,150 for SAE in Harian Jogja looks like a digit transposition of 1,510.
- Province count from the lists: Kalimantan Barat 8 (RKA, SKM, BSLT, ANI, BOS, AER, SAE, API), Kalimantan Timur 2 (STP, JMS), Kalimantan Selatan 1 (MIB).
- PT AER may be PT Arrtu Energie Resources, and PT RKA may be Rafi Kamajaya Abadi: initials and kabupaten match, but no source on the 9 November announcement says so. Treat as unconfirmed leads and do not print as identification. Mongabay's 2019 article also names PT Sinar Karya Mandiri separately, without linking it to SKM, so SKM stays unresolved.

### Gaps
- GMS: the brief lists JMS/GMS as a disagreement. Every page read here printed JMS; I did not find GMS, so its source is unconfirmed.
- No full legal name for any of the 11 in any 9 November coverage read. Nusron declined to name at least the domestic owners.
- No per-company burned area for 2026.
- Unclear whether SAE's 1,510.2639 ha is the whole HGU or the part to be cut. Pontianak Post presents it as the HGU area; Nusron's quote in Antara just says "seluas 1.510 hektare".
- No company-to-country mapping.

### Inferences

- "Pertambangan" in Antara is probably an error, since these are plantation-permit cases, but the outlets do disagree in print.

### Gaps

## Legal basis and criteria

### Takeaway
The written material cites four instruments, and the 50 percent split is applied as Nusron described it in September: over 50 percent burned means the whole HGU, otherwise only the burned part.

### Cited Findings
- Cited basis: UU 30/2014 on Government Administration; PP 18/2021; Permen ATR/BPN 21/2020 on handling and settling land cases; Permen ATR/BPN 15/2016 on release or cancellation of HGU or Hak Pakai on burned land — [Pontianak Post](https://pontianakpost.jawapos.com/metropolis/2611090089/atrbpn-tertibkan-11-korporasi-terlibat-karhutla-sejumlah-hgu-di-kalbar-dicabut); [Investor.id](https://investor.id/national/461331/nusron-wahid-umumkan-nasib-11-korporasi-terkait-karhutla); [Suara bisnis](https://www.suara.com/bisnis/2026/11/09/190538/nusron-cabut-hgu-perusahaan-singapura-dan-malaysia-yang-terlibat-karhutla).
- [Antara](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5842431/atr-bpn-pencabutan-hgu-11-perusahaan-dibagi-menjadi-lima-kategori) and [detikFinance](https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-8744903/11-hgu-perusahaan-terkait-karhutla-di-kalimantan-bakal-dicabut) cite no regulation.
- Cluster 1 criteria: burned area over 50 percent, repeated fires, no prevention or mitigation facilities, violation of the terms of the granting SK — [detikFinance](https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-8744903/11-hgu-perusahaan-terkait-karhutla-di-kalimantan-bakal-dicabut); [kumparan](https://kumparan.com/kumparannews/11-perusahaan-terancam-dicabut-hgu-imbas-kebakaran-hutan-28XWHbGxb57). The written version says "mismatched facilities and infrastructure" rather than none — [Investor.id](https://investor.id/national/461331/nusron-wahid-umumkan-nasib-11-korporasi-terkait-karhutla).
- Cluster 4: "not over 50 percent" with a facilities mismatch per [Investor.id](https://investor.id/national/461331/nusron-wahid-umumkan-nasib-11-korporasi-terkait-karhutla); "kurang dari 50 persen" spoken per [CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/nasional/20261109141734-12-1424964/pemerintah-cabut-hgu-11-perusahaan-terlibat-kebakaran-hutan).
- Earlier rule statement, Rakor at Graha Marinir Panti Perwira, Jakarta, 7 Sep 2026: under Permen 15/2016, below 50 percent of the HGU burned means cancelling only the burned locations; above 50 percent means the whole HGU can be cancelled; his example was a 5,000 ha HGU with 3,000 ha burned — [InfoPublik](https://infopublik.id/kategori/nasional-ekonomi-bisnis/987908/menteri-atrbpn-peringatkan-pengusaha-hgu-bisa-dibatalkan-jika-lahan-terbakar).

### Inferences
- The Permen 15/2016 logic is the operative test for clusters 1 and 4. Clusters 2 and 3 do not rest on it: they turn on an expired right and an HGU never issued.

### Gaps
- I did not read the text of Permen 15/2016, PP 18/2021 or Permen 21/2020, so article numbers are not confirmed.
- Exactly 50 percent is not addressed in the coverage.
