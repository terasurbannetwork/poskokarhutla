# Rakor evaluasi PBPH dan HGU terkait karhutla, Kemenko Polkam, 9 November 2026: apa kata dan apa yang dilakukan tiap lembaga

Source note for the report writer. All 9 Nov items are same-day coverage unless labelled EARLIER CONTEXT. Per-company HGU detail is left to the separate ATR/BPN researcher. Two sources could not be opened (HTTP 403): Sindonews and Bisnis.com; their content is not relied on. No official Kemenko Polkam, Kemenhut, Polri, or KLH release was retrieved, so every figure below is from press coverage.

Source quality: Metro TV, VIVA, IDN Times, Antara, CNN Indonesia, Investor.id, Pontianak Post, JawaPos, and Katadata are established outlets. Bela Rakyat is a small site that reads like a reprint of the Kemenko Polkam release; it is the only source of the attendee list. Its figures match Metro TV, VIVA, and IDN Times.

## Who chaired and who attended?

### Takeaway
Menko Polkam Dudung Abdurachman led the meeting, not Djamari Chaniago as the research brief assumed. The only attendee list found shows most security and law institutions sent deputies or senior officials, and it does not list the Menteri Kehutanan, Kapolri, Kabareskrim, Panglima TNI, Jaksa Agung, Satgas PKH, or any governor.

## What did the Menteri Kehutanan announce?

### Takeaway
No statement by Raja Juli at this event was found. Kemenhut's position was given as a cumulative total of 32 legal subjects sanctioned (5 revoked, 19 frozen, 8 reprimanded or ordered to restore), with no hectares, and that total matches the count already reported in October.

### Cited Findings
- Kemenhut administrative sanctions on 32 legal subjects: 5 permit revocations, 19 suspensions, 8 written reprimands or forced-recovery orders — [Metro TV, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/k8oCOwJ3-aparat-selidiki-334-dugaan-pelanggaran-pidana-terkait-karhutla); [VIVA, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.viva.co.id/berita/nasional/1942213-93-korporasi-masuk-penyidikan-kasus-karhutla-dudung-tak-boleh-ada-yang-kebal-hukum); [Bela Rakyat, 9 Nov 2026](https://belarakyat.com/dudung-abdurachman-evaluasi-hgu-dan-pbph-jadi-langkah-pemerintah-cegah-karhutla-berulang/); [20detik, 9 Nov 2026](https://20.detik.com/detikupdate/20261109-261109036/video-menko-polkam-ungkap-241-orang-dan-93-korporasi-terjerat-karhutla)
- Dudung said HGU and PBPH are not absolute rights, and that proven violations can bring suspension, revocation, or non-renewal — [Metro TV, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/k8oCOwJ3-aparat-selidiki-334-dugaan-pelanggaran-pidana-terkait-karhutla)
- EARLIER CONTEXT (search-result listings, pages not opened): Raja Juli revoked 5 PBPH in Kalimantan in September 2026, with concessions reported at 92,464 ha — [Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/ekonomi-hijau/investasi-hijau/6ab1de94d3dc8/raja-juli-cabut-izin-5-perusahaan-buntut-karhutla-di-kalimantan); [CNBC Indonesia, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/news/20260922150026-4-769920/prabowo-perintahkan-raja-juli-tajam-ke-atas-langsung-cabut-5-izin)
- EARLIER CONTEXT (search-result listings, pages not opened): 6 more companies frozen in Central Kalimantan, with the total reaching 32 companies sanctioned, reported around 21 Oct 2026 — [Liputan6](https://www.liputan6.com/news/read/8318219/izin-6-perusahaan-kembali-dibekukan-buntut-karhutla-ini-daftarnya); [IDN Times](https://www.idntimes.com/news/indonesia/menteri-kehutanan-bekukan-izin-32-perusahaan-terlibat-karhutla-00-rgfwk-qtbmdk)

### Inferences
- The 9 Nov figure of 32 appears to be a recap, not a new action, because the same total was reported in October. The 5 revocations also match the September action.
- The meeting's new permit action was on the HGU side (ATR/BPN). No new PBPH revocation was reported.

### Gaps
- No hectare figure for the 32 sanctioned subjects on 9 Nov.
- No list of the 32, and no count of PBPH evaluated in total.
- Whether all 32 are PBPH holders; the 9 Nov coverage says "subjek hukum".

- The 11 companies in the HGU list are a subset or different cut from the 23 recommended on 29 Oct; no source reconciles them.

### Gaps
- No suspect count, hectare figure, or company-investigated count from Polri on 9 Nov.
- No direct statement by a Polri or Bareskrim official at this event was found.

## What follow-up actions and deadlines were agreed, and what did Menko Polkam conclude?

### Takeaway
Dudung concluded that concessions carry enforceable obligations and that no corporation gets special treatment, and he set four coordination tasks. No dated deadline was reported anywhere; the only timing is Nusron's "segera" for the HGU decree.
