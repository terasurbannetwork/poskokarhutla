# Context, reactions and company identity: 11 Kalimantan HGU companies, 9 November 2026

Research date: 9 November 2026, so post-announcement coverage is only hours old. Tags: [read] means the page itself was fetched. [snippet] means only a search result excerpt or headline was seen, and the claim should be checked before it is quoted. The Gakkum PDFs (gakkum.menlhk.go.id) and the Mahkamah Agung directory could not be opened from this environment, so everything from them is [snippet].

## Timeline in 2026 leading to the announcement

### Takeaway
The 9 November action is the end of an 11-week escalation: Presidential threats from 24 August, a named demand on 7 September that explicitly included HGU, forestry permit revocations on 21 September, and a Bareskrim recommendation on 29 October covering 23 corporations. The brief's "22 Sep" date for the five revocations is the reporting date; the announcement was 21 September, and those five were forestry permits (PBPH), not palm oil HGU.

### Inferences
- The most direct presidential trigger for ATR/BPN involvement is the 7 September instruction, since it is the one that names HGU and ATR/BPN; the 16 September Setkab text speaks only of permits.
- The 11 appear to be the HGU-based subset of the police's 23. Ten of the 11 initials are on the police suspect list (see the identity section), and two of the 21 September PBPH firms also appear to be on it as PT WLJ and PT BWL, which fits the 23 being split by permit type. No source states this split.

### Gaps
- No text of the Bareskrim letter to ATR/BPN was found, nor its date.
- Who holds the post of Menko Polkam needs checking: 7 Sep coverage names Djamari Chaniago, while [Sawit Indonesia, 9 Nov 2026](https://sawitindonesia.com/karhutla-berulang-pemerintah-rekomendasikan-pencabutan-hgu-11-perusahaan-asal-malaysia-dan-singapura) [read] names Dudung Abdurachman. I found no report of the change.
- Kompas TV's text was summarised as dating the Bareskrim conference to 20 October; Kompas and Detik both say 29 October, which is used here.

## Reactions after 9 November

### Takeaway
No statement responding specifically to the 9 November announcement was found from WALHI, Greenpeace, Auriga, Sawit Watch, GAPKI, the DPR, any local government or any of the companies. This most likely reflects timing, since the search ran the same night. The positions below all predate the announcement and show what the groups are likely to press on: naming the companies and their owners.

### Cited Findings

## Prior fire history of these companies

### Takeaway
Four companies have documented fire records before 2026. RKA is the heaviest, with a final Rp 920 billion civil judgment whose payment could not be confirmed; AER has a criminal conviction and is the largest burned concession in one 2026 ranking. MIB also has a conviction on the court directory, but the date and penalty could not be read.

### Inferences
- JawaPos reports that Cluster 1 grounds include burned area exceeding half of the permitted area. The fire figures found here are well under half: SKM about 1,500 ha of 7,716 ha (19 percent) and RKA 2,560 ha of 11,558 ha (22 percent), using HGU areas from [iNews, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.inews.id/news/nasional/nusron-cabut-hgu-3-perusahaan-terindikasi-terlibat-karhutla-total-lahan-capai-22914-hektare) [read]. Either the government counts cumulative or newer burns, or the 50 percent test is not applied uniformly. Companies could use this in an administrative challenge.
- AER's situation stands out: convicted in 2020, and still the top burned concession in early 2026 while, per the government, holding no HGU.

### Gaps
- Execution of the RKA judgment: only the ministry's intention to seize assets was found; no report of payment or seizure. A Gakkum file named "press_release_Karhutla_PT_RKA_250725" suggests a further action in July 2025, but it could not be opened.
- MIB's verdict date, court, and penalty, and whether it relates to 2019 or 2023. The brief's "2019 or 2023" cannot be settled from what was accessible.
- No fire history was found for ANI, BOS, BSLT, SAE, API, STP or JMS before 2026, largely because their names are unresolved or no record surfaced.
