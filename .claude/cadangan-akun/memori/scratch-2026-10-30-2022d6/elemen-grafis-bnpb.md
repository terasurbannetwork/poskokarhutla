On 31 Oct 2026 the owner asked that the BNPB graphic-element catalogue always be pulled in for design work. It lives in three places:
- skill files `bnpb/references/palet-bnpb.md` and `bnpb/references/elemen-grafis.md`, with code in `bnpb/scripts/elemen.py`;
- the artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/H3ScSZeP2yohaA9sdDf4zz;
- repo poskokarhutla `docs/deck-bnpb/Elemen Grafis BNPB 311026/` (PR terasurbannetwork/poskokarhutla#3).

Frame palette: white, navy, orange, blue-grey. Never orange next to red, never biru lembaga next to the data blue. Related: [[ppt-elemen-grafis-gagal]].
