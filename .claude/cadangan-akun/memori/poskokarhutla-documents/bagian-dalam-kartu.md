Owner rule (30 Oct 2026, repeated angrily on /direktori: "mana containernya", "dibuat container"): every section in a profile, sheet, or drawer (Bidang, Kantor pusat, Sumber, Kontak, Terhubung groups, Sejenis, Mengapa partner strategis…) is its own card: white, 1px border, ~14px radius, padding, small uppercase title inside, 12px gap between cards. No horizontal divider lines with large empty gaps.

Related taste from the same day: lists of people/orgs use one compact row component (avatar 40px, name, one grey meta line, max 2 small inline icon pills 20px tall, metrics in fixed narrow columns); hide generic descriptions that only restate name + category ("X adalah organisasi profesi"); action buttons as icons with tooltip, not text; stat blocks fill full width (columns = item count), never leave a gap on the right.

Also (30 Oct, angry): nothing may sit flush against its container ("ga mau ada yang nempel"): every group card needs inner padding in every context (sheet, drawer, profile panel), titles inside the padding.

**Why:** owner judges finish by visual containment and density; loose sections and filler look unfinished.

**How to apply:** tell every UI subagent these rules up front and check screenshots at 1280 and 390 before showing the owner. Related: [[deretan-pilihan-ujung-ke-ujung]], [[aturan-berulang]], [[kepala-situs-wajib]].
