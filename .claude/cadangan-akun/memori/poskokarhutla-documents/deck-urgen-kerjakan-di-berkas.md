When the owner sends a deck and asks for an update ("ubah data yang disini dan tambahkan slide 4 dan 5"), edit that file in place: keep its slides and layout, swap the data, and append new slides in the same visual format. Rebuilding from scratch in another engine made the owner furious ("ngapain lu ulang dari awal? Udah gerak dari sini aja", 26 Oct 2026).

Other rules from that day:
- Presidential karhutla briefing: no cover, no closing slide. The standard pages are the three-slide Laporan Penanganan Pengendalian Karhutla format (peta hotspot, tabel penanganan, operasi), plus extra pages in that same format.
- BNPB air-fleet flight hours (rekap jam terbang) go inside the Operasi slide as a "Jam terbang" column next to the aircraft bars, not on a separate page.
- Never write "Pantau Bencana" anywhere in a BNPB deck.
- Rainy-season page: floods only, specific (per island peak months, kab/kota counts, named provinces). Label tables "berdasarkan data historis". Do not name "Kepala BNPB" as the actor in action items. Do not cite last season's 1,043,740 evacuees.
- "Data terbaru" means today's date. When time is short, use what is already on the web or in cache; do not re-pull satellite or weather.
- The owner sets the cut-off label (e.g. 15.00 WIB even when data is 08.05/14.15). Do it, and state the real data time once.

**Why:** the owner was presenting within the hour. Every rebuild or re-pull cost minutes they did not have, and they asked "berapa lama?" about ten times.
**How to apply:** answer "berapa lama" with a concrete minute estimate. Send an interim file as soon as one exists. Then improve it. See [[aturan-deck-26-okt-rinci]] and [[penanganan-generator-offline]] for the fast 26 Oct data path, [[ppt-tabel-dan-cutoff]], [[laporan-harian-bnpb]].
