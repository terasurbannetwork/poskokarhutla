The owner gave three rules on 26 Oct 2026 for the BNPB deck shown to the Australian Ambassador and NSW RFS:
- **English throughout.** Everything is in English down to the footer, source line, photo credit and unit names ("Inggris aja", "Semuanya sampai ke bawah").
- **No sign of Indonesian or BNPB weakness.** Leave out gaps, unmet targets, hambatan, a one-day extinguished area, "pending verification" and smoke blocking flights. Frame figures as response: tracked, activated, covered by crews, sheltered, housing built.
- **Content, not script.** A slide never carries a spoken line such as "A warm welcome to...". Show the data instead: delegation table, partnership milestones.

**Why:** the audience looks at the slides while the Head of BNPB speaks. Internal briefing material (butir wicara, delegation counts, coordination nota numbers) reads as weak or amateurish to a foreign partner.

**How to apply:** for any deck with a foreign audience, use the engine's English mode: tema `bahasa: en` plus `ui` overrides (proyek-australia is the template). Have a clean-context reviewer read every page for weakness framing before sending. Related: [[ppt-tabel-dan-cutoff]], [[ppt-gaya-kajian-bnpb]].
