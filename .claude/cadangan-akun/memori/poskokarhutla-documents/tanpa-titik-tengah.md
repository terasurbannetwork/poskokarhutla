Never join text pieces with a middle dot "·" (e.g. "263 titik api · 43% padam", "Jawa Tengah · Wonosobo") in decks, cards or UI.

**Why:** Owner said explicitly (20 Okt 2026, paparan Presiden): "Gw ga pernah suka dipisahin titik gini. Catet."

**How to apply:** Use a comma, a plain sentence ("43% dari 263 titik api padam"), its own label/pill, or a new line. Key number first, orange (D9600B) in deck headlines. Owner said it twice on 20 Oct ("Gw ga pernah suka dipisahkan dengan titik gini"). Check every new slide/card text before sending. Related: [[aturan-berulang]], [[gaya-kartu-insight]].
